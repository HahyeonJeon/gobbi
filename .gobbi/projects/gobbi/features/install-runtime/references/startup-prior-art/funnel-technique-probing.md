# The Funnel Technique in qualitative user research

## Insight
Start broad and open, then ask probing follow-up questions that dig into the areas of the response the
researcher wants to learn more about — the probes are generated from the answer, beyond the scripted
questions, and stay scoped to the theme.

## Reason
Grounds the P3 branch-id design: the listed Level-2 branch is the broad opener and the ad-hoc Level-3+ probes
drill into it, scoped to the topic — which is why probes stay under their parent branch (`{branch}.p{n}`) and
inherit the parent's closure rather than becoming new required branches. Invoke when designing the ledger id
scheme and the "follow-ups deepen, do not add required coverage" rule.

## Excerpt

participant's response."
