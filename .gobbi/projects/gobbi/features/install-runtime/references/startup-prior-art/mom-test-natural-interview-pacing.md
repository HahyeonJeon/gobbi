# The Mom Test — natural, unscripted interview pacing

## Insight
Keep customer conversations natural and casual, not formal or over-scripted; jump into conversation mode and
follow the person's answers, and weight past behavior (workarounds, time or money already spent) over stated
interest.

## Reason
Supports P2 — removing the rigid one-question-per-turn cadence and leaving pacing to agent judgment: a fixed
per-turn rhythm is the formal-script posture the source warns against. The startup skill's A1/A2/A5 interview
craft is already built on this source's behavioral-evidence stance, so the pacing removal is consistent with
it, not a deviation. Invoke when justifying the pacing-rule deletion.

## Source
- https://www.momtestbook.com/
- Rob Fitzpatrick, *The Mom Test* (customer discovery)

## Excerpt
"Talk about their life, not your idea ... Don't be formal. Keep it casual ... jump to conversation mode and
ask good questions."
