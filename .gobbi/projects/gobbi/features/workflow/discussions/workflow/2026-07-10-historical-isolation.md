# Historical isolation

## Context
Repository searches find older commands and policy statements that are evidence rather than live defaults.

## Question
Should old histories inform or be rewritten by this model-policy task?

## Options considered
Rewrite every textual occurrence, or classify current versus historical meaning.

## User decision
Do not use old histories as design input. Preserve historical records unchanged.

## Implication
Residual checks classify and exclude historical evidence instead of changing it.
