# PARA — an Area is an ongoing area of responsibility

## Insight
PARA's "Areas" = ongoing areas of responsibility with a standard to maintain — distinct from a Resource (a topic of interest) or a Project (a time-bound effort). An area is something you are responsible for, named per your own context.

## Reason
Confirms gobbi's `{area}` noun is the right word and the right semantic: a project's areas are its ongoing responsibilities (auth, payments, the API surface; for gobbi, its subsystems), which is inherently project-specific. Invoke to validate the naming and the "areas are project-defined responsibilities" framing behind Q1.

## Source
- https://fortelabs.com/blog/para/

## Excerpt
Areas are ongoing areas of responsibility with a standard you need to maintain; they are organized by what they are for, not by topic.
