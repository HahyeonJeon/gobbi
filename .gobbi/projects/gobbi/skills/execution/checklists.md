# Execution Checklist

Mode: evaluation coverage register. Run style: do-confirm. The source stays unchecked. Work a fresh filled copy in one evaluator report. Each applicable row resolves PASS, FAIL with finding IDs, or N/A with inspected evidence. Acceptance requires all applicable rows to PASS.

## Checks

- [ ] **EXEC-CK-01 [GATE] Task and diff fidelity.** Claim: all outputs exist, every hunk maps to the task, and the commit states the actual result. Applicability: unconditional. Pass: task-to-diff and scope checks close. Evidence: final diff, changed paths, commit. On fail: open a Project finding. Sources: X-1, X-4, X-10; EXEC-SC-01.
- [ ] **EXEC-CK-02 [REQUIRED] Consumer usability.** Claim: affected callers, users, operators, and maintainers can use and diagnose the change. Applicability: conditional on affected consumers. Pass: call sites and consumer surfaces are self-explanatory and current. Evidence: cold reads and failure-path inspection. On fail: open a Usage finding. Sources: X-3, X-6, X-10; EXEC-SC-02.
- [ ] **EXEC-CK-03 [GATE] State/data boundaries.** Claim: final behavior preserves invariants across valid, invalid, and exact boundary transitions. Applicability: conditional on state or data change. Pass: direct tests cover applicable edges and prohibited effects. Evidence: targeted results. On fail: open a Structure or Risk finding. Sources: X-5–X-8; EXEC-SC-03.
- [ ] **EXEC-CK-04 [GATE] Structural consistency.** Claim: dependencies remain acyclic and code, tests, types, schemas, validators, manifests, docs, and exports agree. Applicability: unconditional. Pass: caller, dependency, type, and synchronized-surface checks pass. Evidence: direct commands and reads. On fail: open a Structure or Consistency finding. Sources: X-3, X-5, X-6; EXEC-SC-04.
- [ ] **EXEC-CK-05 [REQUIRED] Resource bounds.** Claim: applicable latency, capacity, call count, retry, cache, and cost obligations are measured on the final tree. Applicability: conditional on resource-sensitive change. Pass: measurements meet the locked limits. Evidence: benchmark and resource output. On fail: open a Performance or Cost finding. Sources: X-7, X-8; EXEC-SC-05.
- [ ] **EXEC-CK-06 [GATE] Failure and recovery.** Claim: changed failure paths detect, contain, recover, and guide action without false success. Applicability: unconditional for changed behavior. Pass: injected failures and rollback checks succeed. Evidence: failure tests and error/log review. On fail: open a Risk or Usage finding. Sources: X-7, X-8, X-10; EXEC-SC-06.
- [ ] **EXEC-CK-07 [GATE] Trust and destructive safety.** Claim: validation, authorization, privacy, secrets, dependency trust, and one-way actions remain controlled. Applicability: conditional on matched surface. Pass: direct trust/data/command evidence proves safety. Evidence: targeted checks and authority record. On fail: open a blocking Risk finding. Sources: X-2, X-7, X-9; EXEC-SC-07.
- [ ] **EXEC-CK-08 [REQUIRED] Inclusion and locale.** Claim: applicable accessibility and locale obligations pass outside the default environment. Applicability: conditional on user-facing or locale-sensitive work. Pass: direct tests pass or evidence proves N/A. Evidence: accessibility/locale results. On fail: open a Usage finding. Sources: X-6, X-8; EXEC-SC-08.
- [ ] **EXEC-CK-09 [GATE] Compatibility and reversal.** Claim: existing consumers work or have complete migration and rollback; no stale path remains. Applicability: conditional on changed existing surface. Pass: caller searches, compatibility tests, and reversal proof close. Evidence: searches and migration checks. On fail: open a Consistency or Risk finding. Sources: X-3, X-6–X-9; EXEC-SC-09.
- [ ] **EXEC-CK-10 [GATE] Fresh commit-bound proof.** Claim: exact verification ran on the final committed tree and rejects a cosmetic wrong result. Applicability: unconditional. Pass: checks, commit digest, diff, and status agree; no assertion was weakened. Evidence: fresh command output. On fail: reject completion and open a Project finding. Sources: X-1, X-8–X-10; EXEC-SC-10.

## Closure

Coverage closure requires a terminal evidence result for every row. The task is accepted only when each applicable gate and required item is PASS; a green unrelated suite or executor report cannot replace a failed direct check.
