# Desktop Outcome Projection — Safety and Security Acceptance

This is a subordinate Desktop outcome projection. It cannot replace the Electron security convention,
development, runtime, test, or release skill, and it defines no Electron setting, permission, navigation,
content, session, bridge, fuse, archive, signing, update, or version policy.

Before using it, **MUST load [`electron`](../electron/SKILL.md)** and every applicable child:

- [`electron-convention`](../electron/electron-convention/SKILL.md) owns Electron security boundaries,
  secure defaults, privileged capability contracts, navigation, content, and process ownership.
- [`electron-development`](../electron/electron-development/SKILL.md) governs implementation and review of
  an Electron platform change.
- [`electron-runtime`](../electron/electron-runtime/SKILL.md) supplies pinned-major capability and mechanism
  lookup.
- [`electron-test`](../electron/electron-test/SKILL.md) governs security, trust, rejection, process, and
  packaged evidence.
- [`electron-release`](../electron/electron-release/SKILL.md) governs release hardening, packaging, signing,
  upgrading, update rehearsal, and release artifacts.

## Desktop projection

Desktop owns the product safety question: no product clause may permit a consequence the actor cannot
foresee, refuse, or recover from. Apply that property to the complete outcome, not to a closed list of known
security controls.

Build a product risk and acceptance register with:

- the user action, data, native integration, lifecycle transition, external target, installed state, or
  release consequence at issue;
- the person or data that could be harmed and the harm they would experience;
- what makes the consequence foreseeable, refusable, and recoverable;
- the user-visible denial, cancellation, degraded, and recovery behavior;
- the applicable Electron children and the exact evidence they require;
- the claimed operating systems and artifact states; and
- the direct product observation that proves the person receives the promised outcome.

Do not copy a partial Electron security checklist into this document. Activate the complete applicable rules
in `electron-convention`, implement through `electron-development`, look up mechanisms through
`electron-runtime`, and prove them through `electron-test` and `electron-release`. A green product flow does
not prove a secure boundary, and a green Electron control does not prove that the person can understand,
refuse, or recover from its product consequence.

Exceptions that weaken an Electron protection or create irreversible external effect require the authority
and evidence demanded by the owning Electron child and the Desktop safety decision. Neither record can stand
in for the other.

This projection is complete when the whole product clause inventory passes the Desktop safety property, every
applicable Electron security obligation has evidence from its owner, and the two claim classes remain
distinct in the handoff.
