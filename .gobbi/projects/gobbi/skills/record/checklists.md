# Record Checklist

Mode: evaluation coverage register. Run style: do-confirm. The source stays unchecked. Work a fresh filled copy in one evaluator report. Each applicable row resolves PASS, FAIL with finding IDs, or N/A with inspected evidence. Acceptance requires all applicable rows to PASS.

## Checks

- [ ] **RECORD-CK-01 [GATE] Verdict-specific sealing and ordering.** Claim: the exact PASS output verifies while the matching step or task is still at RECORD, remains valid after its legal completion transition, and is rejected at every earlier stage, non-PASS verdict, wrong task, or future step. Applicability: unconditional. Pass: fixtures cover Ideation, Planning, Execution task and final-step outputs, and Wrap-up with unchanged router bytes during verify. Evidence: before/at/after state, artifact digests, output tree, verifier exits, and byte hashes. On fail: open a Project, Consistency, or Risk finding. Sources: R-1, R-2, R-6, R-9; RECORD-SC-01.
- [ ] **RECORD-CK-02 [GATE] Independent routing proof.** Claim: the manager can reread exact paths, reproduce verification, and derive the next legal route without trusting status text. Applicability: unconditional. Pass: completion proof is complete and current. Evidence: state, artifacts, command, and cold read. On fail: open a Usage or Process finding. Sources: R-1, R-9; RECORD-SC-02.
- [ ] **RECORD-CK-03 [GATE] Atomic owner separation.** Claim: version 5 lifecycle fields change only through checkpoint, version 3 router fields only through transition, and invalid patches preserve exact bytes. Applicability: unconditional. Pass: schema and before/after evidence close. Evidence: fixtures, hashes, and command diagnostics. On fail: open a Structure or Risk finding. Sources: R-7, R-8; RECORD-SC-03.
- [ ] **RECORD-CK-04 [GATE] Canonical tree and placement.** Claim: eager iteration and task structure is complete, artifacts stay in owner slots, and each output is backed by matching RECORD/PASS or the matching completed state. Applicability: unconditional. Pass: tree, task identity, stage, verdict, completed lists, and verifier agree with no symlink, unknown entry, wrong-task output, or future-step output. Evidence: inventory, state fixtures, and verification output. On fail: open a Structure or Consistency finding. Sources: R-2, R-3, R-6, R-9; RECORD-SC-04.
- [ ] **RECORD-CK-05 [REQUIRED] Deterministic bounded operations.** Claim: init, scaffolding, rendering, validation, and verification are idempotent, deterministic, and proportionate. Applicability: unconditional. Pass: rerun hashes and operation evidence agree. Evidence: repeated fixtures and outputs. On fail: open a Performance finding. Sources: R-8, R-9; RECORD-SC-05.
- [ ] **RECORD-CK-06 [GATE] Failure preserves bytes.** Claim: parse, schema, path, identity, and rendering failures change no prior target or sibling file and leave no temporary artifact. Applicability: unconditional. Pass: every failure fixture has exact before/after equality and a precise diagnostic. Evidence: hashes, inventory, status, and corrected rerun. On fail: open a Risk finding. Sources: R-7–R-9; RECORD-SC-06.
- [ ] **RECORD-CK-07 [GATE] Containment and durable boundary.** Claim: all writes stay under the validated session root, staged content is typed and safe, and only Wrap-up may promote. Applicability: unconditional. Pass: canonical-path, symlink, content, and worktree checks close. Evidence: resolved paths, diff, and verifier. On fail: open a blocking Risk finding. Sources: R-3, R-8, R-10, R-11; RECORD-SC-07.
- [ ] **RECORD-CK-08 [REQUIRED] Durable machine and human clarity.** Claim: staged records and canonical outputs validate, render clearly, link meaningfully, and use unambiguous values. Applicability: conditional on written staged files or outputs. Pass: schema/template, outline, cold-read, and link checks pass. Evidence: exact artifacts and validators. On fail: open an Aesthetics or Usage finding. Sources: R-3, R-6, R-9; RECORD-SC-08.
- [ ] **RECORD-CK-09 [GATE] Explicit version and iteration boundary.** Claim: old versions are rejected before mutation, cap extension creates only authorized new directories, and earlier evidence remains unchanged. Applicability: unconditional for version rejection; conditional for cap extension. Pass: schemas, authorization, tree, and hashes agree. Evidence: current/old fixtures and before/after inventory. On fail: open a Consistency or Risk finding. Sources: R-2, R-7–R-9; RECORD-SC-09.
- [ ] **RECORD-CK-10 [GATE] Traceable staging and outputs.** Claim: every staged candidate has durable value and exact evidence, empty staging passes, outputs match evaluated subjects, and no operational exhaust is stored. Applicability: unconditional. Pass: source ledger, digests, verdict/disposition artifacts, tree, and verifier close. Evidence: artifacts and validation. On fail: open a Project, Consistency, or Risk finding. Sources: R-1, R-2, R-3, R-4, R-5, R-6, R-9, R-10, R-11; RECORD-SC-10.

## Closure

Coverage closure requires a terminal evidence result for every row. RECORD is accepted only when each applicable gate and required item is PASS; an assistant report, directory existence, or non-empty staging cannot replace direct schema, byte, and placement proof.
