# Evaluation Operation Review Entry

Use this entrypoint to audit one use of [SKILL.md](SKILL.md). The parent owns the general evaluation method,
perspectives, causal findings, checklist completion, and evidence-derived verdict. This file supplies only
operation-conformance targets. It does not require a workflow, machine schema, renderer, validator, or storage
path.

## Required inputs

- evaluation brief, context record, stable subject identity, and subject inventory;
- independence statement, conflict and competence disclosures, and evidence of an independent reasoning path;
- declared acceptance criteria, verdict rules, aggregation method, and claim-to-evidence plan;
- selected operation and domain scenarios and checks, their results, and the perspective investigation;
- finding ledger, preserve list, completed checklist, limitations, derivation, and final evaluation result; and
- prior subject identity and evaluation when a material revision is in scope.

## Operation-conformance targets

| Review section | Evaluation-operation target |
|---|---|
| Project | The evaluation asks the right bounded question about the complete intended subject and outcome. |
| Structure | Context, criteria, frame, evidence, findings, checks, perspectives, verdict, and handoff form one traceable method. |
| Performance | Subject size, evidence cost, or time pressure changes technique without removing required rigor. |
| Aesthetics | Findings, strengths, evidence, limitations, and reasoning are plain, exact, and easy to scan. |
| Usage | Qualified consumers can reproduce, challenge, and use the result without private or format-specific context. |
| Consistency | Subject identity, criteria, scenarios, checks, evidence, findings, perspective results, and verdict agree. |
| Risk | Independence, uncertainty, missing-evidence behavior, review-only authority, and reevaluation are safe. |
| Overall | The integrated result follows from the complete method and remains independent of caller mechanics. |

## Required operation checks

Run [scenarios.md](scenarios.md) and a fresh filled copy of [checklists.md](checklists.md) in addition to the
subject's applicable domain frame. Verify the stable subject, independence, declared decision rules,
claim-appropriate evidence, prepared-frame completion, perspective investigation, causal findings, coverage
closure, verdict derivation, and bounded handoff. Treat any failed operation gate as an incomplete evaluation
even when the substantive conclusion seems plausible.

## Rule crosswalk

| Parent rules | Primary operation audit |
|---|---|
| “stable, defined subject” and “strongest available evidence” | bounded question, needed expertise, relevant guidance, complete stable subject, and evidence plan |
| “independent evaluator,” “full finding ledger,” and “evaluation separate from correction” | independence, disclosures, provenance, and review-only authority |
| “known evaluation frame” and “complete every applicable scenario and check” | selected, falsifiable, evidence-bearing prepared coverage |
| “search beyond the prepared frame” | complete perspective investigation and evaluated frame expansion |
| “complete causal findings,” “full finding ledger,” and “reproducible” | causal findings, strengths, limitations, and cold-reader trace |
| “derive every result and verdict” | predeclared criteria, completed checks, perspective results, and reproducible verdict |
| “repeat the full applicable evaluation” and “preserve required rigor” | complete current reevaluation without cost-based narrowing |
| “incomplete or invalid result” | exact stop for unresolved evidence, coverage, causality, or derivation |

Every applicable EVAL-CK item appears in the operation audit. This entrypoint evaluates the method and result;
it never repairs the evaluated subject or imposes a caller-specific output contract.
