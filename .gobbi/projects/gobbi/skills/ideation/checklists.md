# Ideation Checklist

Mode: evaluation coverage register. Run style: do-confirm. This is the reusable source; every item is intentionally unchecked. Work a fresh filled copy in the evaluator report. Resolve applicable items only as PASS, FAIL with finding IDs, or N/A with inspected evidence. Acceptance requires PASS for every applicable item.

## Checks

- [ ] **IDEA-CK-01 [GATE] Root outcome and scope.** Claim: cause, design, success criteria, and enumerated scope agree. Applicability: unconditional. Pass: direct clause comparison proves alignment and user approval. Evidence: cause chain, scope diff, decision artifact. On fail: open a blocking Project finding. Sources: I-1–I-3; IDEA-SC-01.
- [ ] **IDEA-CK-02 [REQUIRED] Actor usability.** Claim: every material actor has a usable outcome or approved exclusion. Applicability: unconditional. Pass: actor ledger and cold-consumer read close with no gap. Evidence: actor-to-outcome trace. On fail: open a Usage finding. Sources: I-1, I-7; IDEA-SC-02.
- [ ] **IDEA-CK-03 [REQUIRED] State and data behavior.** Claim: valid, invalid, and exact boundary transitions preserve stated invariants. Applicability: conditional on state or data. Pass: the state/data model covers every applicable edge and prohibited side effect. Evidence: state and boundary trace. On fail: open a Structure or Risk finding. Sources: I-7, I-8; IDEA-SC-03.
- [ ] **IDEA-CK-04 [GATE] Owned acyclic design.** Claim: each concern has one owner and consumer-readable acyclic interfaces. Applicability: conditional on multiple components. Pass: dependency and ownership maps contain no cycle or shared hub without control. Evidence: project-pattern comparison and interface read. On fail: open a Structure finding. Sources: I-5, I-7, I-9; IDEA-SC-04.
- [ ] **IDEA-CK-05 [REQUIRED] Resource bounds.** Claim: scale, dominant cost, external-call behavior, limits, and measurement are explicit. Applicability: conditional on variable work or recurring resources. Pass: estimates and exact limits are present with a measurement commitment. Evidence: budget and call-count review. On fail: open a Performance or Cost finding. Sources: I-6, I-7; IDEA-SC-05.
- [ ] **IDEA-CK-06 [GATE] Failure and recovery.** Claim: each material failure is detected, contained, recoverable, owned, and diagnosable. Applicability: conditional on dependencies, persistence, interruption, or partial mutation. Pass: injected-failure review finds no unhandled state. Evidence: failure matrix and operational obligations. On fail: open a Risk finding. Sources: I-7; IDEA-SC-06.
- [ ] **IDEA-CK-07 [GATE] Trust and governance.** Claim: trust, authority, privacy, retention, destructive action, and license surfaces are controlled. Applicability: conditional on any matched surface. Pass: direct boundary and authority evidence proves safe rejection and compatible use. Evidence: data/trust flow, user decision, license source. On fail: open a blocking Risk finding. Sources: I-3, I-4, I-7; IDEA-SC-07.
- [ ] **IDEA-CK-08 [REQUIRED] Inclusion and locale.** Claim: applicable access and locale needs are designed and testable. Applicability: conditional on user-facing or locale-sensitive behavior. Pass: each applicable need has an obligation; N/A requires inspected false applicability. Evidence: access/locale ledger. On fail: open a Usage finding. Sources: I-7; IDEA-SC-08.
- [ ] **IDEA-CK-09 [GATE] Compatibility and reversal.** Claim: existing consumers, migration, rollback, and one-way gates are explicit. Applicability: conditional on existing users, data, files, APIs, or behavior. Pass: before/during/after and failure states are covered. Evidence: compatibility matrix and rollback plan. On fail: open a Consistency or Risk finding. Sources: I-2, I-7, I-10; IDEA-SC-09.
- [ ] **IDEA-CK-10 [GATE] Traceability and Planning readiness.** Claim: a cold planner can resolve every source, decision, obligation, check, name, and deferred item without private context. Applicability: unconditional. Pass: orphan, placeholder, link, and cold-read checks pass. Evidence: trace ledger and cold-reader result. On fail: halt Planning handoff and open a Project or Consistency finding. Sources: I-4, I-6, I-8–I-10; IDEA-SC-10.

## Closure

Coverage closes only when every row in the filled copy has inspected evidence and a terminal value. Ideation is accepted only when every applicable gate and required row is PASS; a complete list containing a failure is not accepted.
