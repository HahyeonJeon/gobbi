# Ideation Checklist

Mode: evaluation coverage register. Run style: do-confirm. This is the reusable source; every item is intentionally unchecked. Work a fresh filled copy in the evaluator report. Resolve applicable items only as PASS, FAIL with finding IDs, or N/A with inspected evidence. Acceptance requires PASS for every applicable item.

## Checks

- [ ] **IDEA-CK-01 [GATE] Root outcome and scope.** Claim: cause, design, success criteria, and enumerated scope agree. Applicability: unconditional. Pass: direct clause comparison proves alignment and user approval. Evidence: cause chain, scope diff, decision artifact. On fail: open a blocking Project finding. Sources: Rules “problem and boundaries,” “genuine alternatives,” “requested solution or visible symptom,” “scope open-ended,” and “cosmetic alternatives”; IDEA-SC-01.
- [ ] **IDEA-CK-02 [REQUIRED] Actor usability.** Claim: every material actor has a usable outcome or approved exclusion. Applicability: unconditional. Pass: actor ledger and cold-consumer read close with no gap. Evidence: actor-to-outcome trace. On fail: open a Usage finding. Sources: Rules “problem and boundaries” and “complete observable outcome”; IDEA-SC-02.
- [ ] **IDEA-CK-03 [REQUIRED] State and data behavior.** Claim: valid, invalid, and exact boundary transitions preserve stated invariants. Applicability: conditional on state or data. Pass: the state/data model covers every applicable edge and prohibited side effect. Evidence: state and boundary trace. On fail: open a Structure or Risk finding. Sources: Rules “complete observable outcome” and “directional and traceable”; IDEA-SC-03.
- [ ] **IDEA-CK-04 [GATE] Owned acyclic design.** Claim: each concern has one owner and consumer-readable acyclic interfaces. Applicability: conditional on multiple components. Pass: dependency and ownership maps contain no cycle or shared hub without control. Evidence: project-pattern comparison and interface read. On fail: open a Structure finding. Sources: Rules “sufficient evidence,” “parent decisions to dependent details,” “complete observable outcome,” “directional and traceable,” “dependent detail while its parent decision,” and “ordered task plan”; IDEA-SC-04.
- [ ] **IDEA-CK-05 [REQUIRED] Resource bounds.** Claim: scale, dominant cost, external-call behavior, limits, and measurement are explicit. Applicability: conditional on variable work or recurring resources. Pass: estimates and exact limits are present with a measurement commitment. Evidence: budget and call-count review. On fail: open a Performance or Cost finding. Sources: Rules “load-bearing assumption and risk” and “complete observable outcome”; IDEA-SC-05.
- [ ] **IDEA-CK-06 [GATE] Failure and recovery.** Claim: each material failure is detected, contained, recoverable, owned, and diagnosable. Applicability: conditional on dependencies, persistence, interruption, or partial mutation. Pass: injected-failure review finds no unhandled state. Evidence: failure matrix and operational obligations. On fail: open a Risk finding. Source: Rule “complete observable outcome”; IDEA-SC-06.
- [ ] **IDEA-CK-07 [GATE] Trust and governance.** Claim: trust, authority, privacy, retention, destructive action, and license surfaces are controlled. Applicability: conditional on any matched surface. Pass: direct boundary and authority evidence proves safe rejection and compatible use. Evidence: data/trust flow, user decision, license source. On fail: open a blocking Risk finding. Sources: Rules “sufficient evidence,” “genuine alternatives,” “complete observable outcome,” “assumption or weak source,” and “user-owned material choice”; IDEA-SC-07.
- [ ] **IDEA-CK-08 [REQUIRED] Inclusion and locale.** Claim: applicable access and locale needs are designed and testable. Applicability: conditional on user-facing or locale-sensitive behavior. Pass: each applicable need has an obligation; N/A requires inspected false applicability. Evidence: access/locale ledger. On fail: open a Usage finding. Source: Rule “complete observable outcome”; IDEA-SC-08.
- [ ] **IDEA-CK-09 [GATE] Compatibility and reversal.** Claim: existing consumers, migration, rollback, and one-way gates are explicit. Applicability: conditional on existing users, data, files, APIs, or behavior. Pass: before/during/after and failure states are covered. Evidence: compatibility matrix and rollback plan. On fail: open a Consistency or Risk finding. Sources: Rules “problem and boundaries,” “complete observable outcome,” “approved decisions and constraints,” and “silently remove”; IDEA-SC-09.
- [ ] **IDEA-CK-10 [GATE] Traceability and Planning readiness.** Claim: a cold planner can resolve every source, decision, obligation, check, name, and deferred item without private context. Applicability: unconditional. Pass: orphan, placeholder, link, and cold-read checks pass. Evidence: trace ledger and cold-reader result. On fail: halt Planning handoff and open a Project or Consistency finding. Sources: Rules “sufficient evidence,” “load-bearing assumption and risk,” “directional and traceable,” “canonical artifacts,” “readiness before a phase transition,” “approved decisions and constraints,” “assumption or weak source,” and “advance with an unresolved material decision”; IDEA-SC-10.
- [ ] **IDEA-CK-11 [GATE] Base and domain ownership.** Claim: one complete Ideation spine routes only applicable specialized decisions to verified domain owners without copying or weakening their procedures. Applicability: unconditional. Pass: the routing register, existence checks, and ownership trace show every applicable domain selected, every irrelevant domain excluded, and every governing document accepted only from verified authority. Evidence: input and routing register, live skill paths, authority assessment, and parent-to-domain trace. On fail: halt design work and open a Structure or Consistency finding. Sources: Rules scope paragraph “base method” and “domain skills”; Procedure 1, 4, and 6–8; IDEA-SC-11.
- [ ] **IDEA-CK-12 [GATE] Validation evidence boundary.** Claim: current evidence and proposed future validation are distinct, and every planned validation carries a followable later test contract without being represented as a produced output. Applicability: conditional on a material assumption or risk that needs later validation. Pass: the evidence ledger and validation plan name the question, method or artifact, participants or environment, pass and fail signals, reopen condition, owner, and later phase, while the artifact inventory proves no planned output is cited as completed evidence. Evidence: evidence ledger, validation plan, artifact inventory, and cold-reader classification. On fail: halt Planning handoff and open a Risk, Usage, or Consistency finding. Sources: Rules “load-bearing assumption and risk,” “canonical artifacts,” and “readiness before a phase transition”; Procedure 4, 8, and 10; IDEA-SC-12.

## Closure

Coverage closes only when every row in the filled copy has inspected evidence and a terminal value. Ideation is accepted only when every applicable gate and required row is PASS; a complete list containing a failure is not accepted.
