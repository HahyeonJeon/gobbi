# Ideation Checklist

Mode: evaluation coverage register. Run style: do-confirm. This is the reusable source; every item is intentionally unchecked. Work a fresh filled copy in the evaluator report. Resolve applicable items only as PASS, FAIL with finding IDs, or N/A with inspected evidence. Acceptance requires PASS for every applicable item.

## Checks

- [ ] **IDEA-CK-01 [GATE] Root outcome and scope.** Claim: cause, design, success criteria, and enumerated scope agree. Applicability: unconditional. Pass: direct clause comparison proves alignment and user approval. Evidence: cause chain, scope diff, decision artifact. On fail: open a blocking Project finding. Sources: Principles “question the user without hesitation until the idea is complete” and “find the best idea by comparing reference-backed alternatives”; Rules “preserve material user authority” and “accepted contract silently”; Procedure 2–3; IDEA-SC-01.
- [ ] **IDEA-CK-02 [REQUIRED] Actor usability.** Claim: every material actor has a usable outcome or approved exclusion. Applicability: unconditional. Pass: actor ledger and cold-consumer read close with no gap. Evidence: actor-to-outcome trace. On fail: open a Usage finding. Sources: Principle “question the user without hesitation until the idea is complete”; Procedure 2 and 6–8; IDEA-SC-02.
- [ ] **IDEA-CK-03 [REQUIRED] State and data behavior.** Claim: valid, invalid, and exact boundary transitions preserve stated invariants. Applicability: conditional on state or data. Pass: the state/data model covers every applicable edge and prohibited side effect. Evidence: state and boundary trace. On fail: open a Structure or Risk finding. Sources: Principle “discuss the design through a hierarchy of topics”; Procedure 6–9; IDEA-SC-03.
- [ ] **IDEA-CK-04 [GATE] Owned acyclic design.** Claim: each concern has one owner and consumer-readable acyclic interfaces. Applicability: conditional on multiple components. Pass: dependency and ownership maps contain no cycle or shared hub without control. Evidence: project-pattern comparison and interface read. On fail: open a Structure finding. Sources: Principles “study trustworthy prior art before designing” and “discuss the design through a hierarchy of topics”; Rule “complete Ideation from this operation and its owned companions”; Procedure 4–7 and 9; IDEA-SC-04.
- [ ] **IDEA-CK-05 [REQUIRED] Resource bounds.** Claim: scale, dominant cost, external-call behavior, limits, and measurement are explicit. Applicability: conditional on variable work or recurring resources. Pass: estimates and exact limits are present with a measurement commitment. Evidence: budget and call-count review. On fail: open a Performance or Cost finding. Source: Procedure 8; IDEA-SC-05.
- [ ] **IDEA-CK-06 [GATE] Failure and recovery.** Claim: each material failure is detected, contained, recoverable, owned, and diagnosable. Applicability: conditional on dependencies, persistence, interruption, or partial mutation. Pass: injected-failure review finds no unhandled state. Evidence: failure matrix and operational obligations. On fail: open a Risk finding. Source: Procedure 8; IDEA-SC-06.
- [ ] **IDEA-CK-07 [GATE] Trust and governance.** Claim: trust, authority, privacy, retention, destructive action, and license surfaces are controlled. Applicability: conditional on any matched surface. Pass: direct boundary and authority evidence proves safe rejection and compatible use. Evidence: data/trust flow, user decision, license source. On fail: open a blocking Risk finding. Sources: Principles “study trustworthy prior art before designing” and “find the best idea by comparing reference-backed alternatives”; Rule “preserve material user authority”; Procedure 4–8; IDEA-SC-07.
- [ ] **IDEA-CK-08 [REQUIRED] Inclusion and locale.** Claim: applicable access and locale needs are designed and testable. Applicability: conditional on user-facing or locale-sensitive behavior. Pass: each applicable need has an obligation; N/A requires inspected false applicability. Evidence: access/locale ledger. On fail: open a Usage finding. Source: Procedure 8; IDEA-SC-08.
- [ ] **IDEA-CK-09 [GATE] Compatibility and reversal.** Claim: existing consumers, migration, rollback, and one-way gates are explicit. Applicability: conditional on existing users, data, files, APIs, or behavior. Pass: before/during/after and failure states are covered. Evidence: compatibility matrix and rollback plan. On fail: open a Consistency or Risk finding. Sources: Principle “discuss the design through a hierarchy of topics”; Rule “accepted contract silently”; Procedure 3 and 8–9; IDEA-SC-09.
- [ ] **IDEA-CK-10 [GATE] Traceability and completion.** Claim: a cold reader can resolve every source, decision, obligation, check, name, and deferred item without private context. Applicability: unconditional. Pass: orphan, placeholder, owned-link, and cold-read checks pass. Evidence: trace ledger and cold-reader result. On fail: reject Ideation completion and open a Project or Consistency finding. Sources: Principle “question the user without hesitation until the idea is complete”; Rule “accepted contract silently”; Procedure 1, 4, and 9–10; IDEA-SC-10.
- [ ] **IDEA-CK-11 [GATE] Self-contained domain coverage.** Claim: one complete Ideation method covers every applicable concern from project evidence and authoritative domain sources without requiring another skill or outside procedure. Applicability: unconditional. Pass: the input and domain register, authority assessment, source-to-decision trace, and cold-load result show every applicable concern covered, every irrelevant concern excluded, and no missing external method. Evidence: input and domain register, authority assessment, source-to-decision trace, and cold-load result. On fail: reject Ideation completion and open a Structure or Consistency finding. Sources: Principles “study trustworthy prior art before designing” and “discuss the design through a hierarchy of topics”; Rule “complete Ideation from this operation and its owned companions”; Procedure 1, 4, and 6–8; IDEA-SC-11.
- [ ] **IDEA-CK-12 [GATE] Validation evidence boundary.** Claim: current evidence and proposed future validation are distinct, and every planned validation carries a followable execution contract without being represented as a produced output. Applicability: conditional on a material assumption or risk that needs future validation. Pass: the evidence ledger and validation plan name the question, method or artifact, participants or environment, pass and fail signals, reopen condition, owner, and execution condition, while the artifact inventory proves no planned output is cited as completed evidence. Evidence: evidence ledger, validation plan, artifact inventory, and cold-reader classification. On fail: reject Ideation completion and open a Risk, Usage, or Consistency finding. Sources: Procedure 4, 8, and 10; IDEA-SC-12.

## Closure

Coverage closes only when every row in the filled copy has inspected evidence and a terminal value. Ideation is accepted only when every applicable gate and required row is PASS; a complete list containing a failure is not accepted.
