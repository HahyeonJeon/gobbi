# CLI Platform Checklist

This reusable unchecked source evaluates one current command execution-platform fact result governed by the
[`cli-platform`](SKILL.md) Manual. It is bound to that Manual's exact tuple, evidence, diagnosis, fact-return,
and refresh contract. Its stable owner prefix is `CLIPLAT`.

The source evaluates bounded fact quality only. Product decisions, terminal presentation preferences,
security controls, TypeScript and Bun mechanisms, process-test execution, packages, delivery, and release
actions remain outside its subject. Every condition stays unchecked and contains no observation, answer,
score, remediation, or verdict.

## Lifecycle Categories

### Design lifecycle

- **Fact question:** turn one terminal, stream, process, pipe, signal, shell, path, text, locale, or OS concern
  into one bounded question.
- **Tuple model:** identify every command, runtime, target, entry, stream, process, text, context, authority,
  and time field that can change the answer.
- **Evidence design:** identify the primary owners, observable evidence layers, exact failure distinction, and
  refresh conditions before a conclusion is possible.
- **Ownership boundary:** separate the fact from every product decision, mechanism, proof, artifact, and
  action that consumes it.

### Development lifecycle

- **Collection:** collect current primary sources, exact metadata, authorized direct observations, and project
  records without merging their evidence classes.
- **Diagnosis:** preserve the observed signal, competing explanations, conflict, limitation, and smallest
  next fact gap.
- **Return:** produce one complete dated record with one bounded disposition and explicit non-decisions.
- **Revision:** invalidate or narrow the fact when its tuple, evidence, or source changes.

### Product lifecycle

- **Consumption:** provide the bounded fact to the applicable CLI decision or coordination owner without
  changing its meaning.
- **Target change:** prevent evidence for one stream, terminal, runtime, OS, shell, locale, or delivery tuple
  from establishing another.
- **Maintenance:** refresh a moving fact before a current downstream judgment and retain prior facts only as
  dated historical records.

## Scenario Hierarchy

### Design lifecycle

- Fact question
  - One named capability or failure
    - `CLIPLAT-SC-DESIGN-QUESTION-01` — The question asks one fact that one disposition can answer.
- Tuple model
  - Complete answer-changing identity
    - `CLIPLAT-SC-DESIGN-TUPLE-01` — The tuple binds all applicable fact fields.
  - Missing or inapplicable field
    - `CLIPLAT-SC-DESIGN-TUPLE-02` — Every field has an exact, Unknown, or justified inapplicable state.
- Evidence design
  - Current primary owner
    - `CLIPLAT-SC-DESIGN-EVIDENCE-01` — The evidence plan starts from the exact current official owner.
  - Evidence-layer separation
    - `CLIPLAT-SC-DESIGN-EVIDENCE-02` — Documentation, API shape, metadata, observation, and project records remain distinct.
  - Expected failure distinction
    - `CLIPLAT-SC-DESIGN-EVIDENCE-03` — The evidence can distinguish absence from a missing prerequisite or unobserved case.
- Ownership boundary
  - Adjacent result
    - `CLIPLAT-SC-DESIGN-OWNER-01` — Every non-fact result routes to its exact owner.

### Development lifecycle

- Collection
  - Exact-tuple observation
    - `CLIPLAT-SC-DEVELOP-COLLECT-01` — An observation is bound to its actual tuple, authority, method, and side effect.
  - Unrun observation
    - `CLIPLAT-SC-DEVELOP-COLLECT-02` — Documentary evidence never impersonates execution.
- Diagnosis
  - Conflicting sources or observations
    - `CLIPLAT-SC-DEVELOP-DIAGNOSE-01` — An answer-changing conflict remains visible and narrows the result.
  - Ambiguous failure
    - `CLIPLAT-SC-DEVELOP-DIAGNOSE-02` — The next fact separates the remaining causes without mutation.
- Return
  - Complete fact record
    - `CLIPLAT-SC-DEVELOP-RETURN-01` — The returned record is independently interpretable.
  - Direct availability
    - `CLIPLAT-SC-DEVELOP-RETURN-02` — Available is no broader than direct current evidence.
  - Direct unavailability
    - `CLIPLAT-SC-DEVELOP-RETURN-03` — Unavailable is not inferred from a nearby failure.
  - Unknown answer
    - `CLIPLAT-SC-DEVELOP-RETURN-04` — Unknown preserves the exact gap and resume condition.
- Revision
  - Evidence becomes stale
    - `CLIPLAT-SC-DEVELOP-REVISE-01` — An answer-changing change invalidates or refreshes the fact.

### Product lifecycle

- Consumption
  - Presentation default consumes a stream fact
    - `CLIPLAT-SC-PRODUCT-CONSUME-01` — A platform fact informs presentation without choosing it.
  - Release or target decision consumes a platform fact
    - `CLIPLAT-SC-PRODUCT-CONSUME-02` — A platform fact remains separate from the downstream judgment.
- Target change
  - One tuple resembles another
    - `CLIPLAT-SC-PRODUCT-TARGET-01` — Cross-tuple inference is rejected.
  - One stream resembles another
    - `CLIPLAT-SC-PRODUCT-TARGET-02` — Per-stream evidence stays independent.
- Maintenance
  - Current fact is required after a moving input changes
    - `CLIPLAT-SC-PRODUCT-MAINTAIN-01` — Refresh or Unknown replaces stale confidence.
  - Prior fact remains useful
    - `CLIPLAT-SC-PRODUCT-MAINTAIN-02` — Historical evidence retains its original date, tuple, and bounds.

- [ ] CLIPLAT-CK-DESIGN-TUPLE-02-03 — Every answer-changing `Unknown` field forces an `Unknown` disposition.

- [ ] CLIPLAT-CK-DESIGN-EVIDENCE-01-06 — A source for another runtime, OS, shell, terminal, or version is not conclusive for this tuple.

### Design lifecycle > Evidence design > Evidence-layer separation > CLIPLAT-SC-DESIGN-EVIDENCE-02

- [ ] CLIPLAT-CK-DESIGN-EVIDENCE-02-04 — Project logs or artifacts are tied to the exact subject that produced them.

### Design lifecycle > Evidence design > Expected failure distinction > CLIPLAT-SC-DESIGN-EVIDENCE-03
