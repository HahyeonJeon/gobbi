# CLI Development Checklist

This reusable unchecked source evaluates one coordination run governed by
[`cli-development`](SKILL.md), frozen for this source at SHA-256
`bc466e5ce96080217ab60954ad90f56a92960d7d9d2a3a643d88627adaf63a33`. Its stable owner prefix is

The subject is an exact scoped change across accepted CLI product results, TypeScript mechanism results,
consumer evidence, release judgment, action authority, action-result handback, observation, and maintenance.
Specialist policy, implementation, type checking, building, testing, packaging, delivery, publication,

## Lifecycle Categories

### Design lifecycle

- **Coordination subject and activation:** bind one outcome, scope, completion claim, excluded paths, owners,
  identities, evidence needs, authority boundaries, and the direct-owner bypass.
- **Owner and dependency model:** assign each decision, fact, mechanism, evidence, artifact, consumer,
  judgment, action, observation, and maintenance result to one owner and dependency chain.
- **Identity and authority separation:** keep package and direct subjects separate and distinguish release
  policy, readiness, authority, action, verification, and observation.
- **Blocker and handoff design:** define safe retained state, first unproved action, exact resume condition,
  cold-reader context, and complete or recoverable terminal states.

### Development lifecycle

- **Accepted-input reconciliation:** assemble current Architecture, Interface, Platform, Security, and early
  Release results for the same subject.
- **Realization and evidence routing:** route TypeScript implementation, typing, toolchain, testing, package,
  and direct-delivery obligations without absorbing them.
- **Result reconciliation and invalidation:** accept exact owner records, detect contradictions, reopen the
  earliest owner, and mark only affected dependents stale.
- **Readiness and action handoff:** preserve per-row readiness, point-of-action authority, factual action
  handback, exact verification, failure, and recovery as separate states.
- **Observation and maintenance:** bind post-action evidence, new scoped changes, support repair, deprecation,
  and retirement to current exact subjects and owners.

### Product lifecycle

- **Supported consumer outcome:** preserve one complete, honest state for each promised tuple and delivery
  identity from accepted change through support judgment.
- **Acquisition and action boundary:** let package and direct consumers progress independently through cold
  handoff, action, verification, and recovery.
- **Failure and resumed use:** retain authoritative state, exact recovery ownership, and independently
  resumable context across contradiction, missing evidence, interrupted action, and post-release failure.
- **Evolution and exit:** coordinate maintenance, deprecation, replacement, support closure, and retirement
  without silently changing unaffected command or delivery rows.
