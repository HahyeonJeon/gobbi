# CLI Development

CLI Development coordinates one scoped, multi-owner change to a line-oriented TypeScript CLI. It joins
current product, platform, security, TypeScript, consumer, and release results into an exact lifecycle state,
then returns a complete result, recoverable blocker, or cold handoff.

This operation activates for work spanning two or more owners or claiming a supported, delivered, released,
maintained, deprecated, or retired CLI outcome. Bounded single-owner work routes directly to that owner.
Coordination records specialist results; it never performs their work or inherits external-action authority.

## Principles

### Coordinate evidence, not specialist work

Each owner decides, implements, proves, judges, or acts inside its own boundary. The coordinator preserves
those results and dependencies without copying their policy or mechanisms.

### Current agreement determines progress

A downstream state is current only while every answer-changing input names the same exact subject and agrees.
A contradiction returns to the earliest owner and makes only dependent results stale.

### Artifact and consumer identities stay separate

A package archive, standalone executable, direct script, or workspace or revision command keeps its own
consumer entry, evidence, readiness, action, recovery, and observation chain.

### Readiness never grants action authority

A release judgment can justify a handoff but cannot authorize credentials, publication, installation,
rollout, rollback, forward fix, telemetry, or another external effect.

## Rules

- **MUST bind one accepted scoped subject before coordination.** Record its outcome, exclusions, completion
  claim, exact owners, target and delivery identities, evidence needs, write boundary, and action authority.
- **MUST route bounded single-owner work directly and activate coordination only for two or more owners or a
  complete lifecycle claim.** Coordination must not become an approval layer for an independent owner result.
- **MUST keep one dependency record for every accepted decision, fact, implementation, artifact, consumer
  result, release judgment, action, observation, and maintenance state.** Each record names its exact subject,
  owner, inputs, result, evidence, limitations, authority, dependents, reopen condition, and next branch.
- **MUST return every contradiction to the earliest responsible owner and mark affected dependent evidence
  stale.** Historical records remain bound to their original subject and never become current by relabeling.
- **MUST keep release policy, readiness, point-of-action authority, external result, verification, and
  post-release observation separate.** A complete-looking summary cannot substitute for any missing state.
- **NEVER implement, type, build, test, package, publish, install, deliver, roll out, roll back, forward-fix,
  use credentials, or perform specialist work merely because this operation coordinates it.** Stop with the
  exact owner, safe state, first unproved action, and resume condition.

## Procedure

### Phase 1 — Bind and route the scoped change

#### 1.1 Fix the coordination subject

- Start from one accepted CLI change. Record the affected people and automation, observable outcome,
  supported and excluded paths, complete and false-complete conditions, and current product authority.
- Bind the command contract version, runtime and target tuples, package-backed and direct delivery identities,
  consumer entries, compatibility boundaries, required evidence, retained state, and write boundary. Keep
  absent optional delivery forms explicitly out of scope instead of inventing obligations.
- Classify requested credentials, publication, installation, promotion, rollout, rollback, forward fix,
  telemetry, network access, or other external effects. Record each separately authorized owner and stop
  before any action whose subject or authority is absent.
- Continue when the subject and authority boundary are exact. Otherwise return the first missing scope,
  identity, owner, or authority as a blocker with the current safe state and exact resume condition.

#### 1.2 Apply the activation and bypass rule

- List every judgment, fact, realization, evidence, artifact, support, action, observation, and maintenance
  owner required by the accepted outcome.
- Route work that needs one independent owner directly to that owner and stop this operation. Examples include
  one Bun compiler lookup at `typescript-toolchain` and one unchanged warning-expression choice at
  [`cli-interface`](../cli-interface/SKILL.md).
- Activate this operation when at least two owners must agree or the requested result claims the CLI is
  supported, delivered, released, maintained, deprecated, or retired. Load every applicable owner; never
  retain an owner only to make the coordination look complete.
- Record the activation reason, applicable owners, bypassed owners with reasons, and first owner request.
  Return to Step 1.1 if routing exposes an unaccepted scope or ownerless obligation.

### Phase 2 — Establish accepted inputs and dependencies

#### 2.1 Collect product decisions and current facts

- Receive the mechanism-free semantic contract from
  [`cli-architecture`](../cli-architecture/SKILL.md), expression from `cli-interface`, current execution facts
  from [`cli-platform`](../cli-platform/SKILL.md), assurance from
  [`cli-security`](../cli-security/SKILL.md), and early release policy from
  [`cli-release`](../cli-release/SKILL.md) whenever their triggers apply.
- Require each result to name its exact subject, owner, inputs, output or disposition, evidence identity and
  date, limitations, authority, downstream consumers, reopen condition, and next branch. Platform facts keep
  their Available, Unavailable, or Unknown vocabulary and never become support judgments here.
- Compare command, target, delivery, consumer, compatibility, and evidence identities across the returned
  results. Preserve independent package and direct rows even when they share one logical command contract.
- Continue only with current, agreeing inputs and an accepted early release policy. Return a contradiction to
  the earliest product owner, or create a blocker when an answer-changing input is missing or Unknown.

#### 2.2 Route realization and evidence requests

- Give the accepted command and assurance contracts to
  [`typescript-development`](../../typescript/typescript-development/SKILL.md) and
  [`typescript-typing`](../../typescript/typescript-typing/SKILL.md). Give type-checking, stripping, module,
  build, Bun, executable-compilation, and final-map questions to
  [`typescript-toolchain`](../../typescript/typescript-toolchain/SKILL.md).
- Give process-boundary arguments, stdin, stdout, stderr, exit, signal, failure, cleanup, and exact consumer
  behavior to [`typescript-testing`](../../typescript/typescript-testing/SKILL.md). A source-entry result is
  not package or direct consumer evidence.
- Give package-backed command metadata, exact archives, installed package consumers, registry methods, and
  registry recovery to [`typescript-packaging`](../../typescript/typescript-packaging/SKILL.md). Give
  standalone executables and other direct non-archive units, installations, direct consumers, rollback
  rehearsal, and recovery to
  [`typescript-cli-delivery`](../../typescript/typescript-cli-delivery/SKILL.md).
- Record one dependency chain per artifact and consumer entry. Continue when every in-scope owner accepts the
  request; otherwise preserve the declined or unavailable obligation as a blocker rather than doing the work.

### Phase 3 — Reconcile returned owner results

#### 3.1 Validate each result against its subject

- Accept a returned result only when it names the requested contract, implementation or unit identity,
  runtime and target tuple, delivery form, consumer entry, evidence or decision date, result, failure signal,
  limitations, authority, and reopen condition that apply.
- Keep implementation, toolchain output, command evidence, package archive, direct unit, installed consumer,
  and release judgment as separate states. A build, metadata field, source run, target name, or nearby tuple
  cannot fill a missing consumer state.
- Compare each result with its direct inputs and dependents. Record it as current only when the subjects agree;
  record failed, unavailable, unsupported, or blocked results literally without converting them into success.
- Continue when every required current row has a result or exact blocker. Return incomplete or cosmetically
  compliant records to their owner with the first missing field.

#### 3.2 Reopen contradictions and stale dependents

- When results disagree, identify the first decision, fact, implementation, artifact, consumer, or judgment
  whose correction would remove the complete contradiction. Return the conflict and evidence to that owner.
- Mark every result that consumed the changed subject stale. Keep an unrelated row current only after its
  subject and dependency comparison proves that the change cannot alter its answer.
- Preserve stale records as historical evidence with their original identity, date, result, and limitation.
  Never rewrite, relabel, or borrow them for the repaired subject.
- Resume from the earliest reopened owner. Require a new identity for every changed downstream result and
  repeat affected reconciliation before progressing.

#### 3.3 Preserve a recoverable blocker

- Create a blocker when a required owner, fact, environment, evidence result, exact identity, compatible safe
  state, or authority is missing, stale, inaccessible, mismatched, or contradictory.
- Record the exact subject, received inputs, missing obligation and owner, affected claims, retained safe
  state and effects, first unproved action, required authority, risk, and exact resume condition.
- Keep unaffected delivery rows and results separate. Never narrow accepted scope, borrow another form's
  evidence, or label a missing answer as success or Release Unsupported.
- Stop the affected chain at the blocker. Resume only when the same subject remains current and the named
  owner returns the missing result; otherwise return to the earliest changed step.

### Phase 4 — Judge readiness and cross the authority boundary

#### 4.1 Reconcile late release readiness

- Give `cli-release` the accepted policy plus current Architecture, Interface, Platform, Security,
  implementation, toolchain, artifact, exact consumer, compatibility, recovery, and risk-acceptance records.
- Receive one Supported, Unsupported, or blocked judgment per exact promised tuple, delivery identity,
  install state, artifact or revision, and consumer entry. Keep a hybrid's package and direct rows separate.
- Validate the judgment's policy version, evidence links, decision owner and time, limitations, stale triggers,
  safe state, and handoff. A green summary without those identities returns to Release.
- Continue to Step 4.2 for any outward action. A Supported judgment completes readiness only; Unsupported or
  blocked rows retain their exact consumer and recovery disposition.

#### 4.2 Stop for point-of-action authority or issue a cold handoff

- Name the proposed action, exact artifact or revision, consumer or destination, method, owner, credentials or
  access boundary, expected before and after state, verification, recovery, and current readiness row.
- Recheck separate current authority for that exact action. Readiness, a prior action, available credentials,
  or coordinator access never supplies it.
- When authority is absent, return a cold handoff that names the subject, safe retained state, first unproved
  action, required authority, owner, evidence package, and exact resume condition. Include no credential or
  private session assumption.
- When authority exists, hand the action to its specialist owner. This operation performs no action and does
  not record it as attempted until the owner returns a factual action result.

#### 4.3 Reconcile action-result handback and verification

- Receive the action owner's exact subject, actor or automation, authority, time, method, destination, before
  and after states, result, retained objects or effects, failure, limitations, and recovery handoff.
- Record action state separately from readiness. On failure or partial state, stop further affected progress
  and return recovery to the action owner without inventing a retry, rollback, or forward fix.
- Request fresh identity and consumer verification from the applicable TypeScript evidence and delivery
  owners. Mark the action verified only when their exact target results agree with the returned external state.
- Keep every other delivery row unchanged. Continue to observation only for verified rows; retain unacted,
  failed, partial, recovered, or unavailable rows literally.

### Phase 5 — Observe, maintain, deprecate, and retire

#### 5.1 Bound post-release observation

- Enter only with an accepted observation subject. Record exact artifact and consumer rows, existing disclosed
  signals, owner, time window, redaction, retention, stop conditions, comparison rule, and evidence limits.
- Ask the observation owner for the bounded result. Do not enable telemetry, collect new data, query a network,
  or broaden retained diagnostics merely because observation is coordinated.
- Treat a report from another version, tuple, delivery form, or consumer as a lead, not current evidence. A
  contradiction reopens the earliest responsible product, fact, realization, evidence, delivery, or release
  owner and makes its dependents stale.
- Record the verified row as observed only when the bounded result is current. Otherwise preserve the exact
  blocker or incident handoff and safe state.

#### 5.2 Coordinate maintenance and a new scoped change

- Bind every maintenance request as a new scoped subject and repeat the activation rule. A bounded fact,
  wording, compiler, package, or direct-unit task bypasses this operation when it has one owner and no complete
  lifecycle claim.
- Reuse a prior owner result only when its owner proves that the new subject and changed dependencies cannot
  alter it. Mark every other dependent result stale and repeat it through its owner.
- Coordinate support repair from the earliest contradicted owner. Preserve unaffected tuples and delivery
  forms rather than widening or withdrawing them silently.
- Record the maintained state, current evidence, remaining limitations, next review trigger, and owner. Return
  to Step 3.3 when a required maintenance result is blocked.

#### 5.3 Coordinate deprecation and retirement

- For deprecation, bind the exact command contract, runtime, target, delivery identity, artifact, consumer
  population, replacement, compatibility window, acquisition and update behavior, data and configuration,
  recovery, support owner, and end condition.
- Keep semantic command deprecation with `cli-architecture` and runtime, target, artifact, installation, data,
  update, support, or delivery-form deprecation with `cli-release`. Load both when the accepted change crosses
  both axes; route package and direct mechanisms to their TypeScript owners.
- For retirement, require current owner results for obtainability, installed or runnable state, retained data,
  configuration, credentials and diagnostics, uninstall or cessation, recovery limits, final support, and the
  exact terminal condition.
- Record deprecated or retired only when all required results agree. An outward withdrawal, uninstall,
  cessation, or credential action still returns to Step 4.2 for separate authority.

#### 5.4 Close the coordination result

- Reconcile every in-scope row with its current subject, owner, inputs, evidence, limitations, authority,
  dependencies, reopen condition, and next branch. Confirm package and direct identities were never merged.
- Complete only when each accepted row has a current supported, unsupported, blocked, handed-off, verified,
  observed, maintained, deprecated, or retired coordination state and no required work is hidden or deferred.
- Return the activation reason, scoped subject, owner and dependency map, current and stale records, blockers,
  release judgments, action and verification states, observation, maintenance, deprecation or retirement
  state, safe retained effects, limitations, and exact next action.
- State every specialist mechanism and external action not performed by this operation. If one in-scope row
  lacks a terminal or recoverable state, return to its earliest responsible step instead of calling the
  coordination complete.
