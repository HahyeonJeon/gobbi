## Product

- Which signal lets consumers or operators judge scheduled or continuous output complete, fresh, and fit for use?

- After late or corrected input, how can a consumer distinguish the original, current, and revised result?

- During replay, backfill, or reprocessing, what output remains truthful and usable?

- How does a consumer identify, coexist with, migrate to, or safely refuse a Product data or schema version?
