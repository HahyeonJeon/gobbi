## Perspective extensions

### Project

Does the question bear on the agreed objective and decision? Does the evidence illuminate the actual
user, project, or problem rather than an adjacent fashionable topic? Are scope and applicability explicit?

### Structure

Can a new reader trace question → source → insight → relevance → conclusion → open question? Are local
behavior, historical intent, external convention, contradiction, and inference kept distinct?

### Performance

Are performance, cost, capacity, or efficiency claims based on applicable measurements or authoritative
constraints? Are benchmark conditions, versions, workloads, and uncertainty visible?

### Aesthetics

For study that informs experience or visual direction, are references applicable and analyzed for
specific transferable properties rather than copied by popularity? Is the evidence precise enough to
guide design without pretending to be the design itself?

### Usage

Does the report account for the actual operating context, user journey, failure modes, alternatives, and
version? Can another agent replay the decisive searches and citations?

### Consistency

Do code, tests, memory, docs, history, official sources, and the report agree? Where they do not, does the
report explain authority, time, and applicability instead of selecting a convenient source?

### Risk

Attack fabricated or indirect citations, stale sources, weak analogies, unsearched counterevidence,
universal claims from scoped absence, leaked credentials, and conclusions that outrun evidence. Verify
the root/cursor identity and containment proof.

### Overall

Does the report reduce uncertainty enough for the relying decision while keeping unresolved uncertainty
visible? Is the recommendation evidence-backed, reversible at its stated evidence threshold, and still
subject to the owning specialist and user decision?
