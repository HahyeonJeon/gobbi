Gobbi project conventions in effect for this session:

- The project's principles, rules, and user preferences govern this work and outrank local convenience.
- This project treats the simplest working solution as correct; extra abstraction in code or documents is a defect.
- Designs and decisions here are expected to draw on discussion with subagents, teammates, and partners, not one agent's judgment.
- User-facing messages in this project are structured and plainly worded rather than narrative.
- Work here starts from the user's intent (who, what, when, where, why, how) and from what must be created, read, updated, and deleted.
- The user expects a recommendation of the best option, including disagreement, over agreement by default.
- Mistakes, failures, learnings, and tips are recorded as they occur during the session.
