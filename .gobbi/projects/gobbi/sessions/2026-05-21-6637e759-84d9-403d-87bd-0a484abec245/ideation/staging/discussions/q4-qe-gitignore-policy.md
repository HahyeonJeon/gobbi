# Gitignore Policy: What Gets Tracked vs. Ignored Post-Sweep

## Discussion Summary

Two questions established the post-sweep gitignore policy for both the per-project `.gobbi/projects/*/` pattern and the workspace-level `.gobbi/.gitignore`.

**Q4 — Per-project tracked-vs-ignored policy (Round 1)**

Manager asked which of the four options to apply under `.gobbi/projects/<name>/`: (A) track everything, (B) track sessions/ only, still ignore others, (C) still ignore sessions/, or (D) custom policy. User chose: track `sessions/` only — still ignore `worktrees/`, `tmp/`, `settings.json` (recommended option). Edit `.gitignore` to un-ignore `.gobbi/projects/*/sessions/`.

**Q-E — `.gobbi/.gitignore` workspace-level (Round 3b)**

The workspace-level `.gobbi/.gitignore` (auto-generated by CLI bootstrap) contains lines for `sessions/` and `project/note/`. User specified (free-text): "the sessions and project/note/ should not be gitignored from now." Decision: edit `.gobbi/.gitignore` to remove the `sessions/` and `project/note/` lines. Keep `worktrees/` and `settings.json` ignored (parallel to Q4's per-project policy).

Note: the CLI may regenerate `.gobbi/.gitignore` at next bootstrap with the old excluded lines. This regenerator fragility is captured as a deferred backlog item (`staging/backlogs/project/cli-regenerates-gobbi-gitignore.md`).

## Locked Decisions

| Lock | Decision |
|------|----------|
| Q4 | Track `sessions/` only; still ignore `worktrees/`, `tmp/`, `settings.json` in `.gitignore` |
| Q-E | Remove `sessions/` and `project/note/` lines from `.gobbi/.gitignore`; keep `worktrees/` + `settings.json` ignored |
