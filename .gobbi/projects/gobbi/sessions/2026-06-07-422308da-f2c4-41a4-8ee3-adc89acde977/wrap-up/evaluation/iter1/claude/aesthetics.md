# Aesthetics Perspective — Wrap-up promotion + handoff (iter1, claude)

## Frame
Prose quality of the promoted memory and handoff: are the docs self-contained for a zero-context reader (§4.1), free of load-bearing session coordinates in evergreen bodies (§4.3), and written plainly (Principle 7)?

## What I verified
- **Mistakes read self-contained.** Both name their subject in the title, state the situation in their own words, and give concrete commands in `## Correct approach`. They DO reference finding IDs (F-C1, S-1, COD-OVERALL-002) and iter markers in the `## What happened` body — these are descriptive narrative of what occurred, not load-bearing coordinates a reader must resolve to act; the actionable content (Correct approach / How to detect) stands alone. Acceptable per §4.3 (provenance-as-narrative, not load-bearing).
- **Layer-2 prose** is the cleanest of the set: a claim-type→authoritative-source table makes the discipline instantly usable. Self-contained.
- **Journal** is a strong notes doc — per-loop narrative, decisions-to-respect, next-session pointers. Reads end-to-end without the session.
- **Handoff** is tight, tabular, scannable. Shipped table cites task/file/SHA/subject; Decisions-to-respect table cites records.
- **Plain language** throughout; no metaphor, minimal filler.

## Findings
None at a gating level. Minor stylistic note (non-finding): the mistakes' `## What happened` sections lean on evaluator finding-IDs more than strictly necessary; a fully §4.3-pure body could push those to a `## Source` footer. This is the exact subject of one of the deferred backlogs' sibling concern and does not rise to a finding here — the docs remain readable cold.

## Verdict
PASS
