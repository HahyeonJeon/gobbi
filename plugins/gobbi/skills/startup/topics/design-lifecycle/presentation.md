# Presentation Design Lifecycle Overlay

Select this overlay only when accepted evidence identifies a slide-based or sequenced presentation. It covers
visual narrative and delivery adaptation without owning the Product claims being presented.

## Project

- [presentation-identity-range] Which presentation settings must share a recognizable Project identity, and where may tone differ?

## Product

- [presentation-audience-setting] Who is present, what do they know, and under which live, remote, recorded, or self-read setting will they engage?

- [presentation-takeaway] What single conclusion, decision, or action should the audience retain after the presentation?

- [presentation-narrative] Which sequence of context, tension, evidence, explanation, and resolution best supports that takeaway?

- [presentation-slide-role] What distinct purpose does each slide or visual beat serve in the narrative?

- [presentation-pacing] Which content density, reveal, pause, and transition pace fits the audience and available time?

- [presentation-projection-legibility] Which distance, display, lighting, resolution, or compression condition sets the legibility floor?

- [presentation-data-visual] Which visual form makes each material comparison, trend, uncertainty, or relationship accurate and clear?

- [presentation-speaker-context] Which meaning belongs in the visible slide, speaker support, or independent-reading version?

- [presentation-accessibility] Which captions, transcripts, reading order, contrast, text alternatives, or nonvisual equivalents are required?

- [presentation-adaptation] Which narrative and visual decisions must survive changes in duration, venue, audience, presenter, or export format?

## Implementation

- [presentation-delivery-package] Which slide, note, font, media, linked asset, and alternate-format contents must the handoff include?

- [presentation-rehearsal-evidence] What rehearsal or exported-output evidence proves that pacing, media, type, layout, and accessibility survive delivery?
